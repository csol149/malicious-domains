# Contributing

## Submitting a domain

1. Open a [Submit a domain](../../issues/new?template=add-domain.yml) issue, or open a pull request that edits `domains.txt`.
2. Provide evidence, such as public scan or sandbox links and a short description of what was observed.
3. Pick the category that fits best.

## Entry rules

- Domain names only, in the form `||domain.tld^`.
- Lowercase, no scheme, path, port, or IP addresses.
- Check that the domain is not already listed.
- Do not submit domains without evidence.

Sorting and the version date are handled automatically after merge, so you do not need to reorder the file.

## Removal requests

Open a [Report a false positive](../../issues/new?template=false-positive.yml) issue with the reason and any supporting information.

## Review

Maintainers review submissions and may ask for more evidence before merging.
